Beautiful Bangladesh, anytime Right now it is Monsoon

Six Seasons. 700 Rivers. One Delta.

Most countries get four seasons. Bangladesh counts six, and turns into a different country for each one. Come in July and the forests are underwater. Come in January and the same land is gold, dry and full of birds.

    The circulatory system

    The country is a river

    Every line on this map is a real, named river, drawn from survey geometry. Watch the pulse: that is the direction the water goes, off the Himalaya, across the delta, into the Bay of Bengal. It does this on a schedule, every year, and the whole country is arranged around it.

    Things you can only do here

    Nine reasons to come now

    01

    Track a swimming tiger

    Sundarbans tigers cross tidal channels between islands. Three day boat safaris out of Khulna and Mongla, with forest department guides.

    Read more →
    02

    Drink seven layers of tea

    In Srimangal a man worked out how to layer seven densities of tea in one glass. It should not hold together. It does.

    Read more →
    03

    Paddle a flooded forest

    Ratargul is a freshwater swamp forest that spends the monsoon under 20 to 30 feet of water. You go in by rowing boat, through the branches.

    Read more →
    04

    Watch a hundred oars hit at once

    Monsoon boat racing. Boats up to 30 metres long, villages on the banks, drums setting the stroke.

    Read more →
    05

    Watch cloth being drawn by hand

    Jamdani weaving near Dhaka is on the UNESCO intangible heritage list. Two weavers, one loom, six months for a single sari.

    Read more →
    06

    Eat hilsa where it is caught

    The national fish, and an argument. Chandpur claims the best. Go in the monsoon, eat it with mustard, and take a side.

    Read more →
    07

    Count winter birds by the thousand

    From November the wetlands of Sylhet fill with migratory ducks and waders off the Central Asian flyway. Bring a boat and a flask.

    Read more →
    08

    Commission rickshaw art

    Dhaka's rickshaw painting joined the UNESCO list in 2023. The workshops in Bangshal will paint you anything, including you.

    Read more →
    09

    Walk in the new year parade

    Bengali new year, 14 April. Giant papier mache birds, elephants and suns carried through Dhaka by art students since 1989.

    Read more →
    Moving art gallery

    A million canvases, all of them in traffic

    Rickshaw painting entered the UNESCO intangible cultural heritage list in December 2023. Dhaka has roughly a million rickshaws. Do the maths.

    Explore the food of Bangladesh

    Ten dishes worth building a trip around. Rice, river fish, slow beef, and a sweet course the country takes seriously.

    Five journeys, start to finish

    01 / 10 days

    The Whole Delta

    Dhaka, paddle steamer to Barisal, floating markets, Kuakata, back up through Khulna to the Sundarbans.

    See itinerary     02 / 6 days

    Tiger, Tide and Mangrove

    Khulna to Mongla, three nights on a forest boat, Kotka and Hiron Point, ending at the mosque city of Bagerhat.

    See itinerary     03 / 8 days

    Above the Clouds

    See itinerary     04 / 5 days

    Tea, Swamp and Wetland

    See itinerary     05 / 9 days

    Four Thousand Years
